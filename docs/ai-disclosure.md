## What AI did not do

- The AI did not invent facts. Every fact, date, coordinate and image in the app is traced to a NASA page in
  `docs/research-sources.md`. Where no verified value was found, the app shows "Not available".
- No AI-generated images are used. All images are NASA photographs.
