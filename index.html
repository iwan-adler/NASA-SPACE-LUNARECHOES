Humanity explored. History remained. Discovery continues.

When humanity explores another world, what responsibility do we have for the things we leave there?

On the Moon and on Mars, landers, rovers and science stations still stand where people sent them. Most went silent long ago. One still answers when scientists shine a laser at it. Investigate the evidence, then decide what future explorers should do.

The challenge · Abandoned but not Forgotten

Explore the past. Investigate the present. Shape the future.

Since 1967, NASA has sent landers, rovers and science stations to the Moon and Mars. Most fell silent long ago, but the hardware is still there. This museum tells their stories from NASA's own records, shows how planetary science works, and asks what future explorers owe the places we visit.

Every machine is an echo of a mission

What was left

Landers, rovers, a lunar car and science stations on the Moon and Mars, placed there between 1967 and 2018.

Why it matters

Each one was built for a purpose, did its job, and taught us something about another world before it fell silent.

How we know

Every date, place and status comes from NASA's NSSDCA catalogue and NASA mission pages, linked on every story.
