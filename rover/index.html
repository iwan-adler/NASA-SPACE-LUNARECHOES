      How to play
      • Drive with the arrow keys or W A S D, or the on-screen pad.
      • Pulsing markers are sample sites. The scanner points to the nearest one.
      • Press E near a site to collect it.
      • Open the lab (L) to run instruments, read the sample report and answer reasoning challenges.
      • Low on energy? Press R to recharge with the solar panels. There is no way to lose.
